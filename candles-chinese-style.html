CHINESE ARTISTRY

Chinese Artistry

Mahjong symbols, blue-and-white landscapes, the Four Gentlemen and bamboo forms bring Chinese visual culture into a refined candle collection, paired with tea, incense, woods and luminous florals.

From the social ritual of a Mahjong table to blue-and-white porcelain and the symbolic plum, orchid, bamboo and chrysanthemum, each design translates a familiar Chinese motif into a contemporary object for the home.

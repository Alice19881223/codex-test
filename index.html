WHO WE ARE

The essence
of Lanarve.Thoughtful fragrance.
Crafted for you.

Lanarve (LanXin Ningbo Technology Development Co., Ltd.) is a professional fragrance product manufacturer based in Ningbo, China — one of the world’s leading port cities and a major hub for international trade.

With over 10 years of expertise in candle manufacturing and fragrance development, we specialize in the design, production, and supply of premium home fragrance products, including scented candles, reed diffusers, and other aromatic lifestyle products.

Combining creative design, advanced manufacturing capabilities, and deep knowledge of fragrance formulation, Lanarve provides OEM and ODM solutions for global brands, retailers, and boutique businesses seeking high-quality, distinctive home fragrance collections.

# Fragrance display audit — 2026-10-16

The Smells Like presentation changed; fragrance names, descriptions, note arrays and product associations were preserved.

## Verified

- 287 product pages: highlight card exists; displayed description and top/heart/base notes match the selected fragrance.
- 39 fragrance selector cases: description, key notes and all three note layers update together.
- 39 fragrance collection pages: all three note layers match the central fragrance data.
- No display mismatches or browser JavaScript errors observed; representative mobile view has no horizontal overflow.
- 150 products have suggested fragrance associations. 137 designs have no assigned recommendation and continue to ask visitors to choose a direction; they do not display an invented default formula.
- The 34 supplied profiles were reviewed against the original proposed-note table; the remaining five are Mugwort & Incense, Green Tea & Cedarwood, Oud & Saffron, Incense & Myrrh and Pine & Cedarwood.

This verifies consistency of the site's suggested fragrance profiles. It does not establish the actual formulation of a product: supplier formula/specification sheets have not been supplied.
